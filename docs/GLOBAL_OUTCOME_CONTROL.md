# Global Outcome Control

PDC v0.1.0-beta.2 adds a non-negotiable rule:

> A collection of local optima is not necessarily a global optimum.

The full operational rule is part of the Skill at:

- `skills/product-development-controller/references/global-outcome-control.md`
- `skills/product-development-controller/references/strategic-workpath.md`

## What changes in practice

Before materially improving an implementation, experiment, laboratory, test system, architecture, process, metric, or tool, PDC checks:

1. the final Outcome;
2. the current route position;
3. how the action changes progress or a real product decision;
4. the minimum credible quality or evidence threshold;
5. the imperfections and shortcuts that can safely remain;
6. when to stop or switch methods.

A locally imperfect detour may be correct when it is the best credible route to the Outcome. A locally excellent improvement may be wrong when it only improves a proxy while the product route stalls.

## Preview experiments

Preview exists to obtain decision-sufficient evidence, not to build a flawless research laboratory. PDC may use a rough, manual, artificial, or otherwise imperfect experiment when the dimensions causal to the answer remain credible and the limitations are explicit.

The absence of an ideal experiment environment does not prove that the product direction is impossible when a lower-fidelity or alternative experiment can still answer the Primary Unknown.

This rule never bypasses safety, privacy, compliance, data integrity, irreversible-operation, specialist, or frozen Engineering boundaries.
